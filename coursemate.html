CourseMate

CourseMate is your direct channel to share feedback about your academic experience – that’s teaching, learning, assessment, academic support, or wider course experience. Your Course or Senior Rep receives your feedback and works with the University to make improvements based on your recommendations.

Your feedback matters. It’s how we make real changes to improve your experience, for you and for the students who come after you.

Sign in to Student Dashboard →

After you sign in, select CourseMate from the left-hand menu in your Student Dashboard.

Why academic representation matters

Academic representation is at the heart of what we do. Our Rep system ensures every student has a voice in decisions that affect their learning.

Your Course or Senior Rep acts as a bridge between you and the University. They listen to your feedback, advocate for your needs, and work with academic staff to create real change.

How to submit your feedback

Five steps, from signing in to hearing back about what happened next.

1

Sign in to the Student Dashboard

Sign in to the SU website using your University account on your browser, or access it via the SU app. This means our system knows which course you’re on, so your feedback goes to the right people.

Want to stay anonymous? No problem – your rep won’t see your details.

2

Submit your feedback

Enter a short title and your course feedback, then select Submit. You’ll receive an email confirming we’ve received your feedback.

3

We review your submission

A member of the SU team will review your feedback to check it’s constructive and ready to be shared with your rep.

4

Shared with your reps

Your Course or Senior Rep receives your feedback and works with the University to respond and take action.

5

Keep up with updates

When there’s progress, your submission will be updated in CourseMate, or your rep will email you.

Check the status of your submission

You can view your current and past submissions in CourseMate anytime. As your feedback progresses, the status will change.

Submitted

Your feedback is being reviewed by the SU.

In Progress

Your feedback has been assigned to a Rep who is working on it.

Completed

Your feedback has been resolved. Check this tab to see the outcome and any comments from your Rep.

Find your rep

Not sure who represents your course or school? Search by your course, school, or representative’s name to find out who you can contact directly.
