CourseMate

Share feedback about your teaching, learning, assessment, academic support or wider course experience via CourseMate on the Student Dashboard.

Sign in to Student Dashboard →

After you sign in, select CourseMate from the left-hand menu in your Student Dashboard.

What is CourseMate?

Share course feedback and follow what happens next.

Your feedback will be reviewed and shared with your representatives, who will work with the University to respond.

Once they have an update, you will receive an email from your rep. You can also return to CourseMate to check the progress of your submission.

How to access CourseMate

CourseMate is inside your Student Dashboard. Follow these three steps to open it and submit feedback.

01

Sign in to Student Dashboard

Select Sign in to Student Dashboard on this page and sign in with your University account.

02

Open CourseMate

In the Student Dashboard, find CourseMate in the left-hand menu and select it.

03

Submit your feedback

Enter a short title and your course feedback, then select Submit.

What happens after you submit?

Your feedback is reviewed and shared with your representatives, who will work with the University to respond. You can follow its progress in CourseMate.

1

Submission received

CourseMate records your feedback and you receive an email confirming it has been received.

2

Reviewed by the SU

Reading Students' Union reviews your feedback before it is shared with the relevant representatives.

3

Shared with your reps

Your representatives can work with the University to respond to the feedback and take any appropriate action forward.

4

Keep up with updates

When there is an update, you will receive an email from your rep and can return to CourseMate to check the submission.

Check your submissions

Your feedback stays in CourseMate so you can come back and see its current status and any updates.

Submitted / In Progress

Submitted means your feedback is being reviewed by the SU. In Progress means it has been assigned to a Rep.

Completed

Completed feedback appears in the Completed tab with the outcome and any comments added during the process.

View a submission

Open an individual submission to see when it was reviewed, assigned or updated and which Rep is working on it.

Go to your Student Dashboard

Sign in, then select CourseMate from the left-hand menu to submit feedback or check an existing submission.
