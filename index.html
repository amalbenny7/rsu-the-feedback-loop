The FeedbackLoop

01

Improve your course

Course and Senior Reps are advocates for the students on their course and within their school. Working in partnership with the SU Presidents and the Representation & Insight Team, they ensure that your opinions – both positive and negative – are represented at department and school committees. If there are any issues, they work collaboratively with academic staff to create solutions that work for you.

{embed="the-feedback-loop/.find-your-rep"}

Give feedback

Share feedback about your teaching, learning, assessment, academic support or wider course experience via CourseMate on the Student Dashboard.

Your feedback will be reviewed and shared with your representatives who will work with the University to respond. Once they have an update, you will receive an email from your rep.

Go to CourseMate →
02

Give feedback to your SU

Tell us about your experience of Reading SU. You can send a compliment, raise a concern or suggest an improvement to our services and activities.

This could be feedback about an SU service, something we did well, something that did not work for you, or an event you would like to see continue.

Your feedback will go to the most relevant SU team to review, respond where needed and help us identify wider themes and improvements.

Your Presidents

Your four Presidents are elected by students to represent you, campaign for change and help lead Reading SU.

Each President has their own areas of focus and manifesto objectives, but they work together on the issues that matter to students. Visit their profiles to read their priorities, find contact details and follow their latest updates.
