Hey, I am Ashmit Singh, one of your Reading Students’ Union Presidents and an LLB Law graduate.

A little bit about me: I love football and support Real Madrid. I also follow Formula 1 and UFC, love travelling, and my favourite movie is The Pursuit of Happyness.

In the year ahead, I’m passionate about making sure every student feels heard, seen, and supported, and that’s something I want to carry into everything I do this year. Some of the areas I’m especially excited to work on include improving engagement with our international student community, enhancing SU spaces and the overall campus environment, making the Students’ Union as transparent and accessible as possible, and extending my support to all of our sports clubs and societies.

These are just some of the things I have planned, and I hope to take on many more opportunities and projects throughout the year by listening to your ideas and working together to bring positive change to campus.

At the end of the day, the SU is here for you, and I want to do everything I can to make sure you feel comfortable reaching out whenever you need support, have feedback, or simply want a chat.

If you ever need anything, please pop into the SU and ask for any of us four at the Welcome Desk. I’d love to meet you, hear your ideas, and do my very best to make your experience at Reading the best it can be.
