Hi! I’m Bella, one of your SU Presidents this year! One of the main areas I’m passionate about is the mental health and wellbeing of students, and I’m keen to provide more support and opportunities for the lovely people at UOR. Part of my mission is to ensure there are adequate mental health support spaces on campus and to provide opportunities for student expression outside of academia, while also fostering a closer relationship with the local community!

As a graduated History and English Literature student, it would be ironic if I didn’t enjoy going to museums. I love the beach (Devon gal for life), music, and niche fun facts – so if you see me wandering about on campus feel free to swap fun facts with me!! I am a yapper though, so be warned.

I’m super excited for the year ahead and to work with such an amazing team of people, and to be able to make a positive impact on student life. If you have any questions, queries, or ideas feel free to email me: b.cabrera@reading.ac.uk
