Hello!

I'm James (although most people call me Jam), one of your Students' Union Presidents this year. I've just completed my War, Peace and International Relations degree here at Reading, which I thoroughly enjoyed. Alongside my studies, I served as President of the Politics society for two years, facilitating speakers, talks and events.

Student democracy remains the cornerstone of everything your SU does. Combined with my commitment to transparency, I hope to modernise and strengthen this over the next year. Students deserve to know what their SU is doing for them day to day, as well as how we can support you if you need extra help.

For many students, university is the first time they've spent a prolonged period away from home. At such an exciting new chapter in their lives, it's important that they feel safe both on and off campus, whether they're heading home after a night out or cramming a late-night library sesh. Student safety and welfare will remain my top priority over this next year, and it's something I will not compromise on.

Some of you will only interact with the SU via society events or Wednesday Unions. Others will use our Cave study space, Mojos, Advice Service or even our ping pong tables. Whatever you use your SU for, you're always welcome to come and share your thoughts with us! (We will have biscuits)

I could talk for hours about our vision for the future of Reading SU, but the Marketing team told me to keep this short.

See you in 3SIXTY on Wednesday!

- Jam
