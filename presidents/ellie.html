Hi, I’m Ellie, one of your Students’ Union Presidents for 2026/27, and I’m so excited for the year ahead! Outside of the SU, I’m a huge motorsport fan (Team Leclerc), enjoy sports photography, and love discovering new cafés around Reading.

This year, I’m passionate about improving student health and wellbeing, whether that’s creating better wellbeing spaces on campus, improved anti-spiking measures, or building stronger partnerships with organisations like the Reading Florey Clinic. I’m also committed to closing the experience gap that many students face and making sure every student, no matter their background, has the opportunity to get the most out of university life.

If you ever see me around campus, please come and say hello! Whether you want to chat about Formula 1, share your ideas for improving the student experience, or just catch up, I’m always happy to talk. You can also get in touch with me by email at ellie.wiles@reading.ac.uk.
